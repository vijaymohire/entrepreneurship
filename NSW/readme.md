# NSW Small Business Training

This folder contains my **NSW small-business training materials, practical exercises and supporting samples** developed as part of my preparation for Australian business and government procurement opportunities.

The training helped me develop practical understanding of:

* **Business readiness and planning**
* **Winning Business and government procurement**
* **Proposal preparation and pricing**
* **Business continuity and risk management**
* **Australian supplier-readiness requirements**
* **Invoicing and commercial administration**

The materials are retained as a **learning and working reference**, supporting the development of Bhadale IT's international business, supplier-readiness and QAI commercialization activities.

Where samples contain hypothetical information, they are clearly identified as **demonstration/training examples** and should not be interpreted as actual customer engagements or registrations.

**Training completion:** NSW Small Business Commissioner Courses — September 2026.
